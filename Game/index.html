About the Game

This project is part of the FORRT Open Research Games.

Gameplay is strongly inspired by GuessTheCorrelation.

The dataset is provided by a large community of scholars maintaining the Framework for Open and Reproducible Research Training (FORRT):
FLoRA Database.

Gameplay data (guesses, reaction times) will be shared openly for research purposes, attached only to the nickname you provide.

Recommended Citation:
Röseler, L., & Röseler, J. (2026). Guess the replication [Computer game]. Retrieved from https://lukasroeseler.github.io/GuessTheReplication/
